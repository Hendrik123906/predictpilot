Prediction markets are broken for most people. Creating one takes code, capital, and a team. PredictPilot does it alone — every hour, on Solana.

This is the live dashboard. The agent watches the SOL price, compares it to its one-hour moving average, and opens a new market: will SOL be above the strike when the hour ends?

It seeds each market with fifty mock U S D C of liquidity, then trades its own signal. Momentum up — it buys YES. Momentum down — it buys NO. Sideways — it just provides liquidity and waits.

When the hour ends, the agent resolves the market from the real SOL price at expiry, then claims its L P residuals first and its winnings second — the exact order the pm-A M M reference requires.

Every step is a real transaction on Solana devnet. Market creation, the trade, the resolution, the claim — you can verify each one on Solscan.

The agent runs on its own every two hours. It tops up test SOL and mock U S D C from the faucet when it runs low, and the dashboard reads everything live from the chain. Nothing here is mocked.

So far it has run cycle after cycle on devnet, with every action logged on-chain. It has called markets correctly and banked winnings — and when the signal is flat, it sits out rather than forcing a trade.

Under the hood, it talks to the pm-A M M program on devnet with its own keypair, and confirms every transaction over H T T P — because websockets do not survive the proxy it runs behind. The whole loop is about two hundred lines of Node, and the dashboard is a static site that reads the chain directly.

PredictPilot is built on pm-A M M, the prediction-market A M M, for the Solana Perps and Prediction Markets Hackathon — and for the University Blockchain Conference Hackathon. Autonomous markets, autonomous conviction. Open source, live on devnet.
