How it works

1

Watch SOL, set the strike

Every hour the agent fetches the live SOL price and its 1-hour moving average from public market data, then creates a “SOL > $strike by next hour?” market with 20 mUSDC of liquidity.

2

Trade the momentum signal

Price above the 1h average and rising over 15 minutes → buy YES 10 mUSDC. Below the average and falling → buy NO. Sideways → liquidity only, no trade. Every swap is simulated first.

3

Resolve and claim

At expiry the agent compares the real SOL price to the strike, resolves the market, then claims LP residuals first and winnings second — the order the pm-AMM reference requires, or the claim strands.
